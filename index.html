About this curriculum

Instructor-style learning, structured for practical progress

1

Lesson-based approach

Each topic is a learning unit: objectives, tools, lab exercises, and a short assessment (writeup). Follow units sequentially or pick specialization modules after Level 2.

2

Lab-first methodology

Every technique includes a lab recommendation. Use isolated VMs (VirtualBox / VMware) and vulnerable targets for hands-on practice.

3

Professional output

Produce writeups after each lab. Learn to present findings with evidence, risk rating, and remediation guidance — the skills employers and clients expect.

Curriculum

Structured learning stages & unit contents

Level 1 — Foundations (0–3 months)

  • Networking basics (TCP/IP, ports, DNS, ARP)
  • Linux fundamentals and shell scripting
  • Lab setup and VM isolation
  • Essential tools: Wireshark, Nmap, basic Bash
  • Deliverable: 2 short writeups (lab + reflection)

Level 2 — Core Offensive Skills (3–9 months)

  • Reconnaissance: nmap, subdomain enumeration, OSINT
  • Web app security (OWASP Top 10): manual testing + Burp
  • Password attacks and exploit basics (lab-only)
  • Automation: Python/Bash scripting for tooling
  • Deliverable: 4–6 writeups and a mini-project

Level 3 — Professional Practice (9+ months)

  • Privilege escalation (Linux & Windows)
  • Active Directory fundamentals and attack paths
  • Post-exploitation, pivoting, and persistence concepts
  • Reporting: CVSS, remediation, executive summary
  • Deliverable: comprehensive pentest-style report

Legal & safety

Principles and rules for safe practice

  1. Only test systems you own or have explicit written permission to assess.
  2. Use isolated labs (Host-only or internal network) and avoid production networks.
  3. Keep evidence and logs—document steps and outputs for reporting.
  4. Follow responsible disclosure practices when working with third-party programs.
