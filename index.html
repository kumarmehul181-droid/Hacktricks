⚖️ Legal & Responsible Use

Ethical hacking = permission ke andar. India me cyber law ko samajhna bhi skill hai.

⚠️ Yaad Rakho (IT Act 2000 / BNS cyber sections):

  • Bina written permission kisi ka system/network test karna criminal offence hai — sirf apne systems, labs (TryHackMe/HTB/DVWA) aur CTF platforms par practice karo.
  • Computer source code, data, identity ka misuse — sab punishable hai (section 43/65/66 family).
  • Koi bhi crime/cyber harassment ho to complaint: cybercrime.gov.in · helpline 1930.
  • Ye site sirf education ke liye hai — koi illegal activity ki support nahi.
