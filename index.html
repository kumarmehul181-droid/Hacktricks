Roadmap

Learning phases

P0

OS & Lab Setup

Kali Linux, VirtualBox, Linux terminal basics, and safe lab design.

P1

Networking & Recon

TCP/IP, DNS, Wireshark, Nmap, and attack surface discovery.

P2

Web App Security

OWASP Top 10, Burp Suite, SQLi, SSRF, IDOR, and app testing.

P3

Exploitation

Metasploit, public exploits, enumeration, password attacks, and lab exploitation.

P4

Privilege Escalation & AD

Linux/Windows privilege escalation, Active Directory, BloodHound, and lateral movement.

P5

Wireless, Mobile & API

Wi-Fi security, APIs, Android analysis, and specialization paths.

P6

Practice, Reporting & Career

TryHackMe, HackTheBox, report writing, CVSS, and building a portfolio.

Core tools

What you’ll use regularly

Linux & shell

Linux terminal, Bash scripting, permissions, processes, and system understanding.

Recon

Nmap, Wireshark, dig, theHarvester, Shodan, and Google dorking.

Web testing

Burp Suite, OWASP ZAP, sqlmap, dirsearch, ffuf, curl, and Postman.

Exploitation

Metasploit, searchsploit, John the Ripper, Hashcat, Hydra, and Mimikatz.

Privilege escalation

LinPEAS, WinPEAS, GTFOBins, LOLBAS, BloodHound, and Impacket.

Career growth

GitHub, writeups, CVSS, bug bounty basics, and professional reporting.

Legal note

Use it responsibly.

Everything in this repository is for education and authorized lab practice only. Test only on your own infrastructure, CTF platforms, legal lab environments, or explicitly authorized targets.

Never scan, attack, or test systems you do not own or do not have written permission to assess.
